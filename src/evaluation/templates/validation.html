Predicted versus actual

Each dot is one appearance. The diagonal is a perfect prediction. Above it means overprediction; below it means underprediction.

How errors are distributed

Error = predicted − actual. Negative errors are underpredictions; positive errors are overpredictions. A narrower spread around zero is better.

Performance over time

Daily average actual and predicted values across selected appearances. Individual errors can cancel; use MAE to assess accuracy.

Error over time

Daily MAE measures the size of the misses. Mean signed error shows their direction. Hover to see the number of appearances.

What this report can tell you

MAE is the average absolute miss. RMSE gives large misses more weight. Mean signed error measures bias, not accuracy: positive means overprediction on average. All values use the selected statistic's units, not percentages.

Every chart and metric updates for the selected player and dates. Small selections are noisy. Zoom by dragging, hover for details, and use the chart toolbar to save an image. Double-click a chart to reset its zoom.

Only recorded appearances after 10 previous player games and 10 previous opponent games are included. History resets each season. Earlier completed validation games inform later predictions. Missed games and availability are not predicted.

The opponent feature is not used by this baseline. These charts do not establish its usefulness or the quality of roster decisions. Test-season results are excluded. This file works offline; no account or server is required.
